Perspective

Artificial intelligence is often discussed as a technological revolution.

In reality it is also a governance challenge.

Systems capable of making or guiding decisions must operate inside human institutions — healthcare systems, financial systems, public infrastructure, and education.

Designing AI responsibly therefore requires more than algorithms.

It requires architectures that integrate human oversight, institutional safeguards, and operational reality.

Blackpoint Analytics explores these systems.
