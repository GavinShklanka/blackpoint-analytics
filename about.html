About Gavin

Gavin Shklanka is an early-career business analyst focused on workflow automation, decision support, analytics, and the governed use of AI.

His work sits at the intersection of business analysis, structured system design, and applied analytics. Rather than treating projects as isolated technical builds, he approaches them as workflow and decision problems: how information is collected, how logic is structured, how results are communicated, and how a system becomes usable for real people in real contexts.

Across Blackpoint Labs, his portfolio explores public-interest analytics, forecasting, risk analysis, interface-driven research delivery, and governance-aware AI systems. The emphasis is not only on building functional tools, but on turning complex domains into clearer products, dashboards, and analytical workflows that can support better judgment.

His strongest interests are in environments where clarity, oversight, and interpretability matter: operational systems, analytical reporting, risk-aware platforms, and emerging AI workflows that require structure rather than hype. This makes his work especially relevant to business analysis, process design, decision-support, and governance-conscious innovation.

Blackpoint Labs serves as the public-facing home for this work: a place to present projects not as disconnected repositories, but as case studies in how analytical thinking, workflow logic, and interface design can come together in practical systems.
